# Reverse refusal checklist

Use the current Annex VI form applicable to the case. Wording and numbering can change; verify the form before reporting a numbered ground. This checklist converts public refusal categories into pre-submission questions. It does not claim to reproduce an embassy's internal decision process.

| Refusal category | Pre-submission questions | Most relevant evidence |
|---|---|---|
| False, counterfeit, or forged travel document | Is the passport genuine, valid, signed, undamaged, and consistently identified? | passport and prior passports |
| Purpose and conditions not justified | Does the application explain why, where, when, with whom, and at whose cost? Does accommodation cover every night and match an executable itinerary? | form, cover letter, itinerary, transport, accommodation |
| Insufficient means | Can the applicant lawfully cover travel, stay, and return costs after ordinary commitments? | statements, payslips, budget, sponsor evidence |
| Means not lawfully acquirable | Are funds traceable and free of unexplained temporary deposits or misleading presentation? | statement history and source-of-funds evidence |
| Stay-limit problem | Does the planned stay comply with the applicable 90-in-180 calculation and prior stays? | visas, stamps, travel history, calculator result |
| SIS or security/public-policy/public-health/international-relations ground | Is there a known prior immigration or legal issue requiring professional advice? | official notices and travel history; do not speculate |
| Purpose information unreliable | Do bookings, destinations, dates, companions, and stated motives agree? Are reservations verifiable and still valid? | all trip documents and form |
| Statements unreliable | Are factual declarations consistent across forms, letters, interviews, and prior applications? | application form, cover letter, prior records |
| Supporting documents unreliable, inauthentic, or untrue | Do issuer names, dates, amounts, metadata, and verification details agree? Is any content altered, invented, or AI-hallucinated? | originals and issuer-verifiable records |
| Intention to leave reasonably doubted | Is the planned return supported by current, truthful work, study, residence, care, business, assets, prior compliance, or other individual ties? | employment/study/residence evidence, leave, travel history |
| Insurance inadequate | Does the policy meet the current territory, duration, minimum coverage, medical, emergency, and repatriation rules? | insurance certificate and required wording |
| Advance-application justification missing | If applying at a border or under an exception, is inability to apply in advance proven? | exception-specific evidence |

## Use after a refusal

First record the exact checked boxes and remarks without interpretation. For each checked ground, list all submitted evidence that could relate to it, the weakness detected before seeing the decision, new evidence now available, and facts that remain unknown. A match means the review found a relevant weakness; it does not prove that the officer relied on the same document unless the decision says so.
