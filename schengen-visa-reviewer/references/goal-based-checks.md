# Goal-based review modules

Use these four modules for every full review. The official checklist is a gate outside the profile: a missing verified mandatory item can lower the overall grade even when the four modules are otherwise strong.

## 1. Trip purpose, factual accuracy, and executability

Check:

- every named city, attraction, museum, station, airport, hotel, and route exists under that name and is in the stated place;
- permanent closure, seasonal operation, planned closure, opening day, entry conditions, reservation needs, and whether claimed tickets actually exist;
- flights, rail, metro, bus, ferry, and transfers exist; distinguish a usual route from a specific future service that is not yet on sale;
- door-to-door time includes hotel access, check-out, baggage, waiting, transfer, queue, security, meals, rest, and a proportionate disruption buffer;
- the route matches accommodation and the stated purpose rather than reading like a list of attractions;
- precise times, ticket status, booking status, and reservation claims are supported rather than invented by AI.

Use current primary sources: the operator, attraction, airport, official local transport service, government, or official tourism authority. Use large mapping or booking platforms only as corroboration. Blogs and social posts are experience only.

Classify each fact as:

- **Verified:** current primary source supports it.
- **Partly verified:** the place or route exists, but a future schedule or dated availability is not yet published.
- **Needs correction:** name, address, operating day, route, duration, or reservation claim conflicts with reliable information.
- **Unable to verify:** no reliable source found. Say this; do not assert non-existence merely because search failed.

Record source link and access date. Dynamic facts such as schedules, strikes, closures, engineering work, and opening hours must be checked close to submission or travel. Do not treat a possible future strike as a current cancellation.

## 2. Financial capacity and explainability

Check separately:

- **coverage:** the statement period, required pages, freshness, and account ownership;
- **income continuity:** employer pay, payslips, salary credits, payday, and statement cutoff;
- **source clarity:** savings, sponsorship, business income, transfers, large credits, borrowed funds, and lawful access;
- **affordability:** trip length, transport, accommodation, daily costs, dependants, and available funds;
- **payment reality:** paid, scheduled, refundable, on hold, awaiting payment, expired, or cancelled;
- **cross-document agreement:** declared salary, net pay, payer, budget, currency, and account details.

Do not reduce this module to account balance. A high balance with unexplained recent credits may be weaker than a lower but stable and well-explained history. Do not invent a universal minimum unless an official source states one.

## 3. Reasons and evidence to return on time

Check:

- lawful residence and its validity through the relevant period;
- current employment or study, tenure, approved leave, expected return date, and whether the status remains truthful after resignation or notice;
- business, property, dependent care, family responsibilities, or other concrete continuing obligations when actually documented;
- previous travel compliance and prior refusals where available;
- whether the return arrangement is independently supported rather than only asserted by the applicant.

Assess evidence, not stereotypes. Sex, gender, marital status, age, ethnicity, religion, or another sensitive characteristic must not itself lower the result. A single traveller may benefit from clearer evidence, but single status alone is not a defect.

## 4. Cross-document consistency and reliability

Compare:

- identity, name order, birth date, passport, residence, addresses, phone numbers, and issuer contacts;
- trip dates, Schengen entry and exit, arrival home, leave, insurance, hotel nights, and daily itinerary;
- traveller count, companions, rooms, sponsorship, payer, and relationship claims;
- employer, role, tenure, salary, net pay, bank credits, budget, and payment status;
- destination, main-destination nights, route, transport, hotel locations, and purpose;
- booking state, issue date, expiry, cancellation, payment, and whether a document can still support the claim at submission.

Separate harmless differences, explainable gaps, and material contradictions. A supplied explanation does not automatically solve a conflict; check whether reliable evidence supports it.
