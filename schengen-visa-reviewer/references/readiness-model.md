# Four-dimension readiness model

Rate each dimension independently. Use **weak**, **mixed**, **adequate**, **strong**, or **unable to assess**, followed by one sentence of evidence. Do not convert ratings into an approval percentage or imply statistical calibration.

## 1. Official compliance

Whether the application satisfies the current destination-, purpose-, nationality-, and application-location-specific requirements. A verified mandatory defect caps this dimension at weak until corrected.

## 2. Cross-document consistency

Whether identity, dates, destinations, employment, salary, funding, companions, accommodation, insurance, and stated purpose agree. A material false or irreconcilable contradiction caps this dimension at weak.

## 3. Travel credibility and executability

Whether the itinerary is geographically and temporally possible, reservations are proportionate and verifiable, every night is explained, costs are plausible, and AI drafting has not introduced invented facts or meta-commentary.

## 4. Return-tie evidence

Whether current, truthful evidence supports the applicant's intention and practical reason to leave before visa expiry. Review individual work, study, lawful residence, care responsibilities, business, assets, and prior travel compliance. Single status may trigger closer review as EXPERIENCE but cannot determine the rating by itself.

## Overall readiness

- **Not ready:** any unresolved Critical finding, or a key dimension is weak because essential evidence is absent or materially unreliable.
- **Needs targeted fixes:** no unresolved Critical finding, but at least one High finding or a key dimension is mixed.
- **No material issue found in supplied documents:** no Critical or High findings, required scope is substantially complete, and no key dimension is weak. This is not a prediction of approval.
- **Unable to assess:** too many key documents or facts are missing to apply the model honestly.

Do not average dimensions. State how missing documents limit confidence. Distinguish application readiness from the consulate's discretionary assessment.
