## Seed experience prompts

1. **Statement cutoff and salary timing:** Three months of statements may contain fewer than three salary credits when the latest payday occurs after the statement cutoff. Check payslips, employer letter, payday, credit description, and amounts; suggest a truthful explanation or updated statement. Do not call three credits mandatory without an official source.
2. **Single applicant and return ties:** As practitioner experience, invite a closer review of documented return ties. Do not raise severity from marital status alone and do not apply a gender penalty.
3. **Overnight flights and local dates:** Reconstruct local times to prevent hotel, insurance, leave, and itinerary gaps.
4. **Employment transition:** A recent-looking letter may still be misleading if notice has been given or the job ends before travel. Verify current truth, not only document age.
5. **Dense itinerary:** Check door-to-door feasibility, timed entry, opening days, transfers, queues, meals, rest, and disruption buffer. Density alone is not a refusal reason.
6. **AI drafting artifacts:** Remove prompts, strategy notes, defensive language, invented bookings, and unsupported operational details; preserve truthful applicant-specific content.

Add a new reusable rule only when it changes a future review decision. When later cases contradict a prompt, narrow or retire it rather than accumulating exceptions.
