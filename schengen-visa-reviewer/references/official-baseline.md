# Official baseline: Netherlands tourism and EU refusal grounds

Use these as starting points, not frozen substitutes for a fresh destination- and location-specific check.

## Netherlands tourism checklist

- NetherlandsWorldwide, tourism checklist: https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/checklist-schengen-visa-tourism
- NetherlandsWorldwide, visa application documents: https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/documents-visa-application
- NetherlandsWorldwide, responsible country for multi-country travel: https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/visiting-more-than-one-schengen-country

The general tourism checklist covers, among other items, the application form, travel document, legal residence, social or economic ties, proof of stay, travel and stay costs, travel reservation, and travel medical insurance. Location-specific instructions may add or alter documentary details.

## EU law and refusal form

- Consolidated Visa Code: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2009/810/2024-06-11/eng
- Regulation page containing Annex VI: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX:32009R0810
- Regulation (EU) 2019/1155 amending the Visa Code: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/1155/oj

Article 32 includes refusal grounds concerning unsupported purpose and conditions, insufficient means, inadequate insurance where applicable, and reasonable doubts about supporting-document authenticity or contents, statement reliability, or intention to leave before visa expiry. Annex VI provides the standard notification form; an applicant's checked boxes are case-specific evidence, while the blank categories are public.

## Netherlands refusal and objection information

- NetherlandsWorldwide, refusal, objection, and reapplication: https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/objecting-refuse-application

Treat a refusal notice as a starting point. Map each checked ground and any remarks to specific submitted documents and facts. Do not assume which document caused the decision when the notice does not say.
