# Evidence policy

Assign one label to every substantive finding.

## OFFICIAL

A current requirement or refusal ground verified from an EU institution, destination government, embassy or consulate, or officially appointed application centre. Cite the exact source. If official sources conflict, prefer the destination-specific and application-location-specific current checklist and disclose the conflict.

## DERIVED

A logical result of comparing verified facts, such as a hotel gap between the local arrival date and first check-in, an insurance policy ending before Schengen exit, or different salaries in two submitted documents. Show the compared values so the user can verify the conclusion.

## EXPERIENCE

A non-mandatory practice intended to reduce ambiguity, based on recurring case experience or prudent document review. State that it is not an official rule. Phrase it as a question, explanation need, or strengthening suggestion rather than proof of refusal.

Examples include checking whether an otherwise valid employment letter is recent enough to remain persuasive when no official maximum age is stated, asking a single applicant to make return-tie evidence especially clear, and allowing realistic buffers in a multi-city itinerary. An EXPERIENCE signal does not become a risk finding until it connects to a documented weakness.

## HYPOTHESIS

An intuition or applicant belief that lacks reliable evidence. Do not use it to raise a risk level by itself. State what evidence would be needed to test it and redirect to documentable facts.

## Language rules

- Say “the official checklist requires…” only for OFFICIAL findings.
- Say “the documents conflict because…” for DERIVED findings.
- Say “as a cautious practice…” for EXPERIENCE findings.
- Say “this is an unverified hypothesis…” for HYPOTHESIS findings.
- Never say a single issue “will cause refusal” unless quoting an applicable legal refusal ground and the relevant facts are established.
- Do not call prose “AI-generated” as a fact based only on style. Name the observable artifact instead.
